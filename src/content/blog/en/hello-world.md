## Why this site

I'm an ordinary C++ engineer.

For the past few years, my work has mainly been writing high-performance services on Linux, debugging CUDA kernels, and squeezing models onto embedded boards. Honestly, these tasks gave me a solid technical foundation — but they also made me realize something: **a day job alone can no longer satisfy my hunger for growth and creation**.

This feeling intensified after the AI tooling explosion.

Tool scripts that used to take a week now get done in an evening with Claude Code. Memory leaks that used to take two days to debug now get analyzed — with AI-assisted call stack inspection and fix suggestions — in half an hour. **Productivity has multiplied, but I started asking myself: where should I spend this multiplied time?**

The answer is this site.

## Why not just WeChat / CSDN

I did consider publishing only on WeChat Official Accounts or Zhihu. But I ultimately chose to build my own site, for a simple reason:

> **A personal site is a "digital asset"; a platform account is "rented traffic"**.

When WeChat changes a rule, you might lose half your followers overnight. If CSDN goes down someday, your content is gone. But your own domain and your own content will always be there — **accessible via `buildbyliang.com` 10 or 20 years from now**.

And a bilingual site lets my work reach overseas developers — something a WeChat account can never do.

## Update Cadence

My target is **one deep-dive article per week**.

No fluff, no trend-chasing, no copy-paste. Every post must answer a real question — either a pitfall I've stepped into, a conclusion I've thought through, or a piece of source code I've dissected.

**If I can't write something worth publishing in a given week, I'd rather not post.** Quality always beats quantity.
